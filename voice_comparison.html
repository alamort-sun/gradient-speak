The Trap of the Gaze

You hit on something profound with that distinction between seeing and feeling:

  • Sight is inherently observational. It invites the watcher, the critic, and the metrics of external validation. It is often why people feel constrained — because sight turns pure existence into a performance.

If seeing is believing, feeling is knowing from the inside out. When the world tells us to optimize for what can be measured, seen, or captured, we risk starving the exact parts of ourselves that only respond to resonance, heat, and rhythm.
